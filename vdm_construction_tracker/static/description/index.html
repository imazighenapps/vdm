📖 User Manual — Complete Guide

1

🔧 Installation

Follow these simple steps to install the module on your Odoo 19 instance:

  • Download the module zip file from the Odoo Apps Store.
  • Extract the vdm_construction_tracker folder into your Odoo addons directory.
  • Restart the Odoo server.
  • Go to Apps → Update Apps List.
  • Search for Construction Project Tracker and click Install.
✅ Tip: The module requires project, purchase, stock, account, hr, and mail modules. They are installed automatically as dependencies.
2

🏗️ Creating a Construction Project

This is the foundation of all your construction management. Every BOQ, DPR, billing, and subcontract is linked to a project.

  • Navigate to Construction → Projects → All Projects.
  • Click New to create a project.
  • Fill in: Project Name, Client, Site Manager, Contract Value, Start Date, End Date.
  • Select the Project Type (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Infrastructure, Renovation).
  • Click Confirm to activate the project.
✅ Tip: The project auto-generates a reference number (PRJ-0001, PRJ-0002, etc.) for easy tracking.
⚠️ Important: Once confirmed, you can add phases, BOQs, work orders, and start tracking daily progress.
3

📋 Setting Up Bill of Quantities (BOQ)

The BOQ defines all work items, quantities, and rates for your project. It's the backbone of budget control.

  • From your project, click the BOQ smart button (or go to Construction → Bill of Quantities).
  • Click New and select your project.
  • Add BOQ lines: Description, Work Type, Unit, Quantity, Unit Rate.
  • The Amount is calculated automatically (Qty × Rate).
  • Click Approve when the BOQ is finalized.
✅ Tip: You can create revisions of approved BOQs. The revision number increments automatically.
✅ Tip: Link BOQ lines to phases for automatic progress calculation.
4

📅 Daily Progress Reports (DPR)

DPRs are your daily site records. They track what was done, who was working, and any issues encountered.

  • Go to Construction → Site Management → Daily Reports.
  • Click New and select your project.
  • Fill in: Date, Weather, Temperature.
  • Describe Work Completed in the rich text editor.
  • Enter Labor Deployed, Materials Used, Machinery Used.
  • Record any Safety Issues or Delay Reasons.
  • Write the Next Day Plan.
  • Click Submit to send for approval.
✅ Tip: Select the Phases Worked Today to link the DPR to specific project phases.
5

📸 Site Photos with Geolocation

Visual documentation is crucial in construction. Capture photos with GPS data for accurate site records.

  • From a DPR, go to the Photos tab and click Add a line.
  • Upload the photo, add a Caption, and select the Photo Type (Progress, Quality, Safety, Issue, General).
  • Enter Latitude and Longitude if available.
  • Photos are automatically linked to the project and DPR.
✅ Tip: Organize photos by type to quickly find safety issues, quality problems, or progress shots later.
6

💰 RA Billing (Running Account)

Track monthly billings with retention, advance recovery, and net payable calculations.

  • Go to Construction → Billing → RA Billing.
  • Click New, select the project, and enter the Billing Period.
  • Add billing lines with descriptions, quantities, and rates.
  • Set Retention % (default 5%), Advance Recovery, and Previous Bill Amount.
  • The Net Payable is calculated automatically.
  • Click Submit → Approve → Create Invoice.
✅ Tip: Click Create Invoice to generate a customer invoice in the Accounting module automatically.
7

🔄 Change Orders

Manage variations and scope changes with a formal approval workflow.

  • Go to Construction → Billing → Change Orders.
  • Click New and describe the change.
  • Select the Reason: Client Request, Design Change, Site Condition, Regulation, Error, Other.
  • Enter the Original Amount and Variation Amount.
  • The New Total is calculated automatically.
  • Submit for approval. Once approved, click Implement.
⚠️ Important: Change orders with status "Submitted" appear as alerts on the dashboard, ensuring nothing is missed.
8

👷 Subcontractor Management

Track subcontractors, their scope, retention, payments, and measurements.

  • Go to Construction → Subcontracts.
  • Click New and fill in: Subcontractor, Scope of Work, Contract Value, Retention %.
  • Click Activate to start tracking.
  • Go to the Measurements tab to record periodic measurements with quantities and rates.
  • Track Amount Paid and Remaining Amount automatically.
✅ Tip: The retention amount is calculated as: Contract Value × Retention %. This is held until project completion.
9

📈 OWL 3 Interactive Dashboard

The dashboard gives you a complete overview of all your construction projects at a glance.

  • Navigate to Construction → Dashboard.
  • View stats cards: Total Projects, Active Projects, Total Contract Value, Average Progress.
  • Use Quick Actions buttons to jump to Projects, DPR, BOQ, Billing, Change Orders, or Subcontracts.
  • Check Alerts for overdue projects and pending change orders.
  • Browse the Recent Projects table with progress bars and status badges.
  • Click any project row to open it directly.
✅ Tip: The dashboard updates in real-time. Overdue projects and pending approvals are highlighted automatically.
10

📄 Generating PDF Reports

Generate professional PDF reports for printing and client submission.

  • BOQ Report: Open a BOQ → Click the Print button → Select BOQ Report.
  • DPR Report: Open a Daily Report → Click Print → Select Daily Progress Report.
  • RA Billing Report: Open a billing record → Click Print → Select RA Billing Report.
✅ Tip: Reports include your company header, project details, and formatted tables. Ready for printing or emailing to clients.
11

💡 Tips & Best Practices

Get the most out of Construction Project Tracker with these tips:

  • Start with the Dashboard — It shows everything at a glance. Check alerts daily.
  • Submit DPRs every day — Consistent daily reports prevent disputes and keep records clean.
  • Use phases for progress tracking — Assign weights to phases for accurate overall progress calculation.
  • Link BOQ lines to phases — This enables automatic progress updates from work orders.
  • Capture photos regularly — Visual records are invaluable for dispute resolution.
  • Review change orders weekly — Don't let approvals pile up. The dashboard alerts you.
  • Use the Print button — Generate PDFs for client meetings and official submissions.
  • Multi-company? No problem — Each company sees only its own projects by default.
