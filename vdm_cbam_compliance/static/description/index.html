CBAM Compliance Tracker

Track embedded emissions, manage certificates, and file quarterly CBAM reports
directly from your Odoo ERP — fully compliant with EU regulations.

✓ Plug & Play Odoo 19 Community & Enterprise
⚠️

Non-compliance with CBAM can cost you up to €100 per tonne of CO₂ — and the definitive phase starts January 1, 2026.

The EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism requires all importers of covered goods to report embedded emissions quarterly and purchase certificates to offset their carbon footprint. Manual tracking in spreadsheets is error-prone, time-consuming, and puts your company at risk of significant penalties. Odoo has no built-in CBAM support — this module fills that gap.

Everything you need

🚚

Import Event Tracking

Automatically log every CBAM-covered import with product classification, embedded emissions, supplier data, and country of origin.
🍂

Certificate Management

Track CBAM certificate purchases, surrenders, and remaining balances with FIFO cost-basis tracking. Automated low-balance alerts.
📄

Quarterly Reporting

Generate EU-compliant quarterly CBAM reports with a single click. Pre-filled templates with all required fields. CSV/PDF export.
📊

KPI Dashboard

Live overview of reports due, certificates to purchase, total CO₂ tracked, and cost forecasts. Never miss a deadline.
👤

Supplier Management

Maintain a registry of CBAM suppliers with installation details, sector, and emission data. Request self-declarations directly.
🔔

Deadline Alerts

Automated reminders 30/14/7 days before each quarterly filing deadline. Never miss a CBAM reporting obligation.

How it works

1

Configure your products

Assign CBAM sector and CN codes to your products. Set default emission values when supplier data is unavailable.
2

Track your imports

Each purchase of a CBAM-covered product automatically creates an import event with embedded emissions data.
3

Manage certificates

Purchase CBAM certificates, surrender them against imports, and track your balance in real time with FIFO cost tracking.
4

File your report

Generate the quarterly CBAM report and export to CSV/PDF for submission to the EU Transitional Registry.
