Installation

Up and running in minutes

Follow these simple steps. No developer knowledge required.

1
Install the module

Copy vdm_fleet_management_plus to your addons path, restart Odoo, then go to Settings → Apps and click Install.
2
Add your vehicles

Go to Fleet Management Plus → Vehicles and add your fleet with details, images, and driver assignments.
3
Schedule maintenance

Create preventive maintenance schedules under Fleet Management Plus → Maintenance.
4
Track fuel and costs

Log fuel entries and other costs as they occur.
5
Monitor the dashboard

Open the dashboard to see real-time fleet statistics, upcoming maintenance, and cost analysis.
FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before installing.

Is it compatible with Odoo Community and Enterprise?
Yes. The module only depends on core Odoo apps (fleet, hr, mail, account) and works on both Community and Enterprise editions of Odoo 19.
Does it work with the standard Odoo Fleet module?
Yes! Fleet Management Plus extends the standard fleet module with additional features like maintenance scheduling, fuel tracking, and cost analysis.
Can I track multiple vehicle types?
Yes. Supports cars, trucks, vans, motorcycles, buses, and trailers. Each type can have its own settings and cost configurations.
